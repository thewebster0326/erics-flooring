A LITTLE ABOUT US

Built on Craftsmanship.
Driven by Quality.

Eric’s Flooring has been a trusted name in the construction industry, providing flooring solutions designed to enhance the look, feel and function of interior and outdoor spaces. Based at 48 Mons Street, Bellevue East, Johannesburg, we bring a practical, customer-focused approach to flooring projects, renovations and finishing work.

We understand that flooring is more than a surface beneath your feet. It sets the tone of a room, affects the way a space feels, and must stand up to everyday life. That is why we focus on careful preparation, suitable materials, skilled workmanship and a clean, considered finish.

Whether you are installing new floors, bringing an older timber floor back to life, maintaining a wooden sundeck or refreshing a property, we aim to make the process clear and dependable from the first enquiry through to the final details.

  • Solutions for new builds, renovations and existing properties
  • Installation, maintenance, restoration and repair services
  • Clear communication and attention to the details that matter
  • Available for enquiries 24 hours a day

THE STORY BEHIND ERIC'S FLOORING

A passion for craftsmanship.
A name built on trust.

Every great business begins with a belief in doing things properly. Eric’s Flooring is built around that simple idea: a floor should do more than fill a room — it should bring warmth, character and pride to the space people call home or work.

As the business grew, the focus remained on the details that matter: preparing surfaces carefully, respecting the property, choosing a suitable flooring solution and taking the time to work towards a clean, lasting finish.

For owner Eric, the goal is to give customers a flooring experience they can feel confident about — one built on clear communication, practical advice and workmanship that reflects pride in the trade.

OUR MISSION

Transform residential and commercial spaces through dependable flooring and finishing solutions, combining careful planning, skilled workmanship and attentive service.

OUR VISION

Become a first-choice flooring and finishing partner in Johannesburg and beyond, recognised for craftsmanship, professionalism and lasting client relationships.

OUR VALUES

Quality. Integrity. Customer care. Reliability. Continuous improvement. Every project deserves respect and attention.
